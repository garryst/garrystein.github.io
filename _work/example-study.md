## The problem

Describe the business or user problem in two or three sentences. What was the team worried about, and what decision was coming up?

- What did new users expect to do in their first session?
- Where did they get stuck, and why?
- Which change would help most people finish setup?
{: .questions}

## The method

Explain why you chose these methods over other options.

- **User interviews** 12 new users, recruited from recent sign-ups
- **Usability testing** 8 sessions on the existing setup flow
- **Funnel analysis** Drop-off at each step, with the data science team
{: .cards}

## What I found

> Most participants could not say what the product did after finishing setup. They completed the steps without understanding them.
{: .finding}

Add two or three more findings. Use a short heading or bold lead-in, the evidence (a quote, a number, a screenshot), and what it meant for the team.

- **62%** left before step three
- **9 of 12** couldn't explain the product's purpose
- **3** steps users called "pointless"
{: .stats}

## The impact

State what changed because of your work, with numbers where you have them. If you don't have metrics, describe the decision the team made.

- **Design** Replaced the five-step setup with one starter task
- **Product** Added the setup completion rate to the weekly dashboard
- **Engineering** Cut two setup screens from the next release
{: .cards}
