I'm currently a UX researcher at Grainger, and I've had the chance to work across a few different industries, including video games, cybersecurity, and now B2B industrial supply. My journey into UX research started at the University of Utah, where I studied Game Design and Development. I got to build games from the ground up and work directly with players to understand what was working, what wasn't, and how we could make things better. That experience got me interested in understanding why people interact with things the way they do, which eventually led me to pursue my master's in Human-Computer Interaction at Indiana University.

One of my favorite parts of being a researcher is getting to work with different teams and learn from different perspectives. I enjoy digging into problems, asking questions, and working alongside designers and product teams to figure out how we can improve the experience for users. Working across different industries has also given me the opportunity to understand a wide range of people, their needs, and the challenges they face.

I'm always looking to learn something new, take on different challenges, and continue growing as a researcher. Outside of work, you'll usually find me snowboarding, hiking, or playing video games. I've been into gaming ever since I got my first PS2 when I was seven, and that hasn't changed much!
